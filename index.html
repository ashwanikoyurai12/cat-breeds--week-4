Why are cat breeds different?

Cat breeds vary in coat length, colour, body shape, temperament, and grooming needs. Breed descriptions are general guides: every cat has its own character and care requirements. The breed names and short summaries in this slider are based on Purina UK’s Cat Breed Library.

Source: Purina UK — Cat Breed Library. The photos are illustrative and are not guaranteed to show verified pedigree examples.

Quick Cat Fact

  • Maine Coons are large cats with long, thick coats and tufted ears.
  • Siamese cats are known for their pointed coat pattern and social nature.
  • Bengal cats have a sleek, muscular look and distinctive patterned coats.
